Oathwise

U.S. Citizenship Test study app

Get ready for your U.S. citizenship interview

Oathwise helps you prepare for the naturalization interview in one place:

  • All official civics questions of the 2025 test (128) and the 2008 test (100), with the answers for your state and current officials.
  • Mock interviews that follow the official rules, flashcards and spoken answers.
  • Reading and writing practice for the English test with the official vocabulary.
  • Every Form N-400 yes/no question explained, a guide to the whole process, an eligibility calculator and a trips calculator.
  • In English, Español, Tiếng Việt, فارسی / دری, پښتو and العربية. The test itself is in English; translations help you understand.

No account, no ads, no tracking: your information stays on your phone. Studying every question is free; the optional Oathwise Pro one-time purchase unlocks unlimited practice.
