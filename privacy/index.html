Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026 · Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy explains how the Oathwise mobile app (the “App”) handles information. The App is developed and published by Faiz Dae (“we”, “us”), topjow.org. If you have a question, write to support@topjow.org.

In short: the App has no account or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device. We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The App does not send your progress, answers, trips, case dates, receipt number, settings or any other information you enter to us or to anyone else. We do not use analytics, crash reporting or advertising services, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

2. Information stored only on your device

To work, the App saves the following in its private storage on your device. It is never uploaded by the App.

InformationWhy
Settings: language, state and congressional district, civics test version, daily goal, theme, reminder timeTo show the answers and screens that apply to you
Study progress, bookmarks, test history, document checklistTo track your progress
Trips outside the United StatesTo calculate physical presence and continuous residence
Case dates and USCIS receipt number (optional)For the interview countdown and study plan
Oathwise Pro status and the free practice used todayTo unlock Pro and apply the daily free limits
Downloaded content updates (for example current officials)To keep the study content current

Your answers in the Form N-400 review are not saved; they are cleared when the App closes. You can delete stored information at any time: use “Reset study progress” in Settings, “Delete” in My case, remove a trip, or uninstall the App, which deletes all of it.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone and speech recognition (optional). Used only after you tap a microphone button, to check your spoken answers and your reading practice. The words are recognized by your device’s speech service (Google on Android, Apple on iOS), which may process the audio on its servers under its own privacy policy (Google, Apple). The App does not record, save or send audio to us.
  • Notifications (optional). Used only if you turn on the daily study reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • Internet. Used to download content updates and to open official websites in your browser.

4. Content updates

About once a day the App checks for updated study content (for example new officials after an election) and downloads it from our website, which is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). No personal information is sent with these requests. Like any web server, the host may record technical data such as your IP address in its logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement; we do not receive or use these logs.

5. Purchases

Oathwise Pro is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play or the Apple App Store. The store processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; we never receive your payment details, name or email. The App only remembers on your device that Pro is unlocked, and asks the store to confirm or restore the purchase.

6. Text-to-speech and links

Questions are read aloud by your device’s text-to-speech engine. Links to official websites (such as uscis.gov) open in your browser; those websites have their own privacy policies.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information, because we do not collect it.

8. Children

The App is meant for adults preparing for the U.S. naturalization interview and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Security

Information stays in the App’s private storage on your device and is protected by your device’s security (such as its screen lock and encryption). Keep your device secure and up to date.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information. Because we do not collect or hold personal information, you can exercise control directly in the App as described in section 2. If you have a request or a question, contact us and we will reply within 30 days.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the dates above. If a change affects how the App handles information, we will also say so in the App or in its store listing.
